Grading rules

  • Hit: the week's close-to-close move went the way of the signal (Monday open to Friday close; in holiday weeks, the first to the last trading day). Trading days end at the 5pm New York close, as in the Profiling data. A flat week counts as a miss.
  • Which signal: the one the dashboard shows for the week slot the Monday falls in (days 1–7 = Week 1, 8–14 = Week 2, 15–21 = Week 3, 22 onwards = Week 4).
  • CHOP (and AVOID) isn't graded. FLIP is graded on the second half of the week (Wednesday open to Friday close), after the turn, in the post-turn direction: the following week's BULL/BEAR signal, or else the opposite of the previous week's.
  • Moves are shown as multiples of ADR20 (the average daily range of the 20 trading days before the week), so gold and EURUSD can be compared fairly.
  • The signal that was live at the time is the one graded. Weeks marked LIVE use the weekly snapshot of what the site showed; BACKFILL weeks are graded with today's seasonal data, which already includes those years. Later data edits never rewrite live history.
