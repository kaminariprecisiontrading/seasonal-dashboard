01 · Purpose

One destination for objective confluence

Markets repeat. Harvests, heating seasons, fiscal year-ends and fund flows leave footprints that show up year after year. KPT Seasonals converts those historical tendencies into structured, interactive dashboards — then checks them against price behaviour, the macro calendar and your own data, so a seasonal bias is never taken on faith.

Seasonal context first

Every signal blends 5-year, 15-year and long-term data, broken down from the yearly arc to the month and down to Week 1–4.

Every angle in one place

Seasonality, market profiling, live price, macro events, your own backtest and an AI verdict — side by side instead of across ten browser tabs.

You keep the trigger

The dashboard supplies the data, not the trade. Entries, stops, sizing and management stay with your own execution framework. Read the risk disclosure.

Read a signal in two seconds Shown on every asset card
BULLHistorically, price has tended to rise this week.
BEARHistorically, price has tended to fall this week.
CHOPNo clean directional tendency — timeframes disagree or price ranges.
FLIPThe tendency reverses inside the period — expect a turn.
Conviction dots — five filled means all three timeframes agree. Fewer dots, weaker confluence.
02 · Inside every asset

Seven tabs, seven layers of evidence

Open any asset card and you get the same seven-tab workspace — ordered from broad context down to the final synthesis.

Tab 1
Seasonals

Combined and per-timeframe tables in an accordion. The current month opens automatically, with week-level signals and conviction.

Tab 2
Trend

The cumulative seasonal curve with a NOW marker — see instantly whether the asset is still climbing toward its seasonal peak or already past it.

Tab 3 · 14 assets
Profiling

Statistics from full M1 price history: range distributions, profile shapes, when highs and lows form, NFP effects, plus a forward Range Outlook.

Tab 4
Live Price

An embedded TradingView chart, so you can check where price actually sits against recent structure before acting on a bias.

Tab 5
Macro

An economic calendar pre-filtered to the events that move this asset, with a guide to which releases matter most.

Tab 6
Upload

Drop in any MT5 CSV (M1 to monthly). Get a seasonal backtest — win rate and average return — plus hour, session and weekday bias.

Tab 7
Analysis

AI synthesis across every layer, returning LONG, SHORT, NEUTRAL or WAIT with rationale and a risk note. Claude, Gemini or a local Ollama model; export as .md.

Plus on the index

Search by name, filter Bull / Bear / Other, jump by asset class, and a weekly tally of every signal.

03 · How to use it

From a weekly scan to a reasoned bias

Work top-down. Each step narrows the picture, and any step can tell you to stand aside.

Read the full user guide
  1. 01
    Scan the indexEvery card shows this week's signal. Filter to Bull or Bear, or search an asset by name.
  2. 02
    Open the asset · SeasonalsRead the week's combined signal and conviction, then check neighbouring weeks: is the bias building or fading?
  3. 03
    Confirm on TrendWhere is the NOW marker? A curve already past its peak is a caution flag, even on a BULL week.
  4. 04
    Size and locate the moveProfiling shows typical range and timing; Live Price shows whether price is near a level worth acting on.
  5. 05
    Validate and de-riskUpload your own data to test the win rate, find the best session, and check Macro for events that could override the season.
  6. 06
    Synthesise, then execute your wayRun Analysis for a structured verdict. Entry, stop and size remain your call.
