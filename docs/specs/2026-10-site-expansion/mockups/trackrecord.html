Grading rules

  • Hit: the week's close-to-close move went the way of the signal (Monday open to Friday close, broker time).
  • CHOP isn't graded. FLIP is graded on the second half of the week, after the turn.
  • Moves are shown as multiples of ADR20, so gold and EURUSD can be compared fairly.
  • The signal that was live at the time is the one graded. Later data edits never rewrite history.
