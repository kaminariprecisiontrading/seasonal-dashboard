1. Who runs this site

Kaminari Seasonals Dashboard is a personal project run by G T, an individual based in Botswana who publishes as Kaminari Precision Trading. To the limited extent the site processes personal information, G T is the responsible party under Botswana's Data Protection Act, 2024. Contact: kaminariprecisiontrading@gmail.com.

2. What this site does not collect

  • No accounts, logins or sign-up forms.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • Nothing you type, upload or configure is sent to this site's server.

3. Information stored in your browser

The dashboard remembers some settings using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device; I can't see it. You can clear it at any time from your browser's site-data settings, or with the controls described below.

4. Files you upload

CSV files you load on the Upload tab are read and processed entirely inside your browser. The file is never uploaded anywhere. Only the computed summary is kept, in kpt-up-*, so other tabs can use it.

5. AI analysis

When you click Run Analysis, the asset's seasonal data, plus any statistics from your uploaded CSV, is used to build a prompt. Where that prompt goes depends on the provider you choose:

  • Ollama: sent to the Ollama server you configure, normally on your own computer. It doesn't leave your machine.
  • Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini: sent directly from your browser to that provider using your own API key, and handled under that provider's privacy terms. It never passes through this site.

The prompt contains market data and statistics, not personal details. Please don't add personal information to it.

6. Your API keys

API keys are kept in your browser and sent only to the provider they belong to. You choose whether a key is remembered on this device or kept for this session only, and you can delete keys at any time with Clear keys in AI settings. For extra safety, set a spending limit on any key you use here, and don't save keys on shared computers.

7. Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. That information is handled under Netlify's privacy policy and isn't used by me to track or profile visitors.

8. Third-party content

  • TradingView (Live Price tab) and Investing.com (Macro tab) load only after you click to load them, or if you've chosen Always load. Once loaded, they're served by those companies and may set their own cookies.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the open-source charting and text-formatting libraries the dashboard uses, so your browser contacts it when those load.
  • Fonts are served from this site. [If fonts are still loaded from Google Fonts at launch, replace this sentence with: "Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when pages load."]

9. Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. Third-party cookies can only appear after you load a TradingView chart or the Investing.com calendar. Your choice to load embeds automatically is saved in local storage (kpt-embeds). You can change it at any time with the Embeds setting in the site footer.

10. International transfers

Netlify, Anthropic, Google, TradingView, Investing.com and Cloudflare operate outside Botswana, mainly in the United States and Europe. When you use features that rely on them, your technical information or prompts are processed in those countries, under those providers' safeguards.

11. Your rights

Under Botswana's Data Protection Act, 2024 (and, where they apply to you, laws such as the EU or UK GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete personal information about you, and object to how it's used.

Because the site holds almost nothing about you, most of your data is already under your control: clearing your browser's data for this site removes it. For anything else, email kaminariprecisiontrading@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with the response, you can complain to Botswana's Information and Data Protection Commission, or your local data protection authority.

12. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and uses a content security policy to limit which scripts can run. No visitor data is stored on a server. Keys and settings saved in your browser are only as secure as your device, so take care on shared computers.

13. Children

The site is intended for people aged 18 and over.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
