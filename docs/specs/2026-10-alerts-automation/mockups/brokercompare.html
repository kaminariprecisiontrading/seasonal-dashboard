STATISTICTRADERSWAYDERIVDIFFERENCE
Server time zoneBrokers cut daily candles at different times, which moves highs and lows between days. All feeds are aligned to the 5pm New York close before comparing.
Price source & spreadDifferent liquidity providers and markups shift prices slightly. This matters most for small weekly moves near zero.
History lengthA shorter history means fewer years per week slot, so signals are noisier and conviction is capped. Compare like with like using the overlap period.
