HOW TO READ SYNTHETICS

Deriv's synthetic indices are generated by a random number generator with fixed statistical properties. They aren't driven by economies, news or trading sessions, so there is no known reason for seasonal tendencies to persist. Their signals are produced and graded exactly like any other asset's, and the hit rate is the honest test: a rate that stays near 50% is what randomness predicts.
