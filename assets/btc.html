Methodology: Moore Research Center does not cover crypto, so this page's Seasonal Tendency signal is not hand-authored chart analysis like the site's other 96 assets. It is statistically derived from ~9 years of real BTCUSD daily price history (TradersWay MT5 export, cleaned via the KPT-Market-Profiling pipeline), using the same week-of-month bucketing and Raw Price Tendency calculation the site's own Backtest tab already uses to validate signals elsewhere (60%+ of years positive → LONG, 39%- → SHORT, otherwise CHOP). Because the sample is short, star conviction is capped well below the 5-star maximum used for assets with 30-40 years of data — treat this page's ratings as directional color, not the same strength of signal as the rest of the site. The 15-YR column shows the same full-history computation as the 9-YR column, since BTCUSD does not have 15 years of price history yet.
