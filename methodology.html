Methodology

How a signal is built — and where it stops

Every BULL, BEAR, CHOP or FLIP label on the dashboard traces back to decades of historical price behaviour. Here's the full path from raw chart to weekly signal, and the honest limits of what it can tell you.

From chart to signal in five steps

  1. 01 · SOURCESeasonal chartsMoore Research Center tendency overlays for each futures contract: 5-YR, 15-YR and long-term (up to 40 YR).
  2. 02 · DIGITISEMonths × weeksEach chart is read into a MONTHS[] table: 12 months, weeks 1–4, a direction per timeframe.
  3. 03 · COMBINEOne combined readingThe three timeframes merge into a single weekly reading, rated 1–5 for conviction when the chart is read. The more they agree, the higher the conviction.
  4. 04 · RESOLVEToday's weekWhen the page loads, your clock picks the current month and week from the signals manifest (days 1–7 = Week 1, 8–14 = Week 2, 15–21 = Week 3, 22 onwards = Week 4).
  5. 05 · LABELSignal + convictionBULLBEARCHOPFLIPPlus 1–5 conviction dots.

Why three timeframes

The 5-year view reflects the current regime. The 15-year view smooths out one-off shocks. The long-term view (up to 40 years) captures structural cycles like harvests, heating demand and fiscal year-ends.

When all three point the same way, the tendency has held across very different market eras. When they disagree, the dashboard usually says so (CHOP or FLIP) instead of picking a side.

Conviction is rated by hand as each chart is read, not by a formula, but it tracks agreement closely: across the futures data, 95% of five-dot weeks have all three timeframes pointing the same way.

How forex pairs are synthesised

There's no futures contract for most spot pairs, so each one is built from two currency futures: the base currency read directly and the quote currency inverted. EURUSD = EUR futures (CME) + USD Index (ICE, inverted).

Base ▲ + Quote inv. ▲Double alignmentBoth components push the pair the same way. These are the pair's strongest windows.
Base ▲ + Quote inv. ▼CollisionThe components fight each other, which usually reads as CHOP. Stand aside unless other layers decide it.
Base ▲▲ + Quote inv. ▼▼Maximum conflictBoth components are strong and point opposite ways — the hardest weeks to call. Read as CHOP.

What a seasonal signal is not

Not a forecastIt's an average of past years. This year can and will differ.
Not an entryIt gives directional context only. Price structure and your own framework decide the trade.
Averages hide outliersOne extreme year can bend a curve. Use the Upload tab to check the win rate.
Regimes changeA 40-year pattern may predate today's market structure. That's why the 5-YR view is shown alongside it.
Events overrideA central-bank surprise outweighs any season. Always check the Macro tab.
Weekly resolutionIt says nothing about the time of day. Profiling and the Upload tab cover timing.
